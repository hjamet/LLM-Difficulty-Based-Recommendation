\abstract{
    This research presents an integrated LLM-based system for foreign language learning, combining text difficulty estimation, simplification, and content recommendation. Our difficulty estimation model achieves 18-56\% accuracy improvements over existing methods in classifying French texts. The text simplification component demonstrates effective complexity reduction while preserving meaning through minimal LLM fine-tuning. Our recommendation system integrates these capabilities with graph-based techniques, showing \mm{up to a 17}\% increase in NDCG scores for personalized content suggestions.

    \textcolor{red}{While instantiated and evaluated on French content, our modular architecture can be adapted to other target languages equipped with comparable pre-trained models.} Through extensive evaluation, we demonstrate the effectiveness of both zero-shot and fine-tuned approaches. This research advances natural language processing and educational technology by providing an integrated solution for personalized language learning, offering practical insights into applying LLMs in educational contexts.
}
