\subsection*{Data Availability}
The code used in this research is publicly available on GitHub at \url{https://github.com/hjamet/LLM-Difficulty-Based-Recommendation}. The datasets used for difficulty estimation, text simplification, and public recommendation benchmarks (\texttt{Zeeguu}, \texttt{ml-100k}, and \texttt{Goodreads}) are publicly available on HuggingFace at \textcolor{red}{\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/OloriBern/FLDE}, and the 150-pair human evaluation pool and anonymized annotations (Section~6.2.3) are included in the public repository}. The Tomplay dataset used for the recommendation system evaluation contains private user data and is not publicly shared to protect user privacy. Any requests for access to the private dataset should be directed to the corresponding author and will be considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to data protection regulations.

\subsection*{Ethics Approval}
\textcolor{red}{The human evaluation of text simplification reported in Section~6.2.3 was conducted exclusively by two adult native French-speaking expert evaluators on publicly available literary and educational sentences, with no collection of personal, medical, or sensitive data from external human subjects. In accordance with institutional regulations at the University of Lausanne and the University of Mons, this expert linguistic assessment did not require formal Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval.}
